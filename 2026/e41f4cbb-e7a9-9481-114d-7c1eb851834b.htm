Member nameValueDescription
Disabled1 For a structural column, this disables rigid Link formation, unless the neighboring structural beam overrides the setting. For a structural beam, this disables rigid Link formation, overriding the neighboring structural column's setting.
Enabled0 For a structural column, enables rigid link formation, unless the neighboring structural beam overrides the setting. For a structural beam, enables rigid link formation, overriding the neighboring structural column's setting.
FromColumn2 For a structural beam, this indicates that the structural column setting should be used to determine rigid link formation. This value is not applicable to structural columns.
Remarks
Rigid Links are curves connecting neighboring Structural Beams and Structural Columns.
