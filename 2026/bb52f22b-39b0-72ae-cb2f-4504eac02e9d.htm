layerIdx  Int32
Index of a layer in the CompoundStructure. The layer index is zero based. It counts from the exterior of wall and from the top of roofs, floors and ceilings.

Return Value

StructDeckEmbeddingType
The embedding type of the structural deck associated to the specified layer. Invalid if it is not a structural deck.
