Success0 The load operation was successful.

CannotFindServer4 The server identified by the ExternalResourceReference could not be found, and may not be registered.

Remarks
This flag is returned by the LoadResource method of the IExternalResourceServer class to inform callers whether the load operation succeeded, or encountered an error.
